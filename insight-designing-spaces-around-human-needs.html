Designing spaces around human needs

Why culture, context and function remain important considerations in the design process.

Architecture responds to people, place and purpose. Successful environments balance circulation, usability, safety, atmosphere and a sense of belonging.

Understanding user needs at the beginning of a project can influence layouts, finishes, natural light and opportunities for flexible use.

The best outcomes combine aesthetic character with functional needs and practical long-term performance.
