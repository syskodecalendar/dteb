OUR STORY

About DTEB

A legacy of professional excellence, design innovation and engineering expertise.

ESTABLISHED 2003

A multidisciplinary practice with a human focus.

Dheya Tawfiqi Engineering Consultancy Bureau W.L.L. was established by Dr. Dheya A. Aziz Tawfiqi in Bahrain in 2003.

DTEB's approach brings together planning, architecture, interior design, civil and structural engineering, building services and project delivery consultancy.

The firm supports clients with technical expertise, clear coordination and respect for the cultural context of each development.

Our Vision

Our Mission

Our Strategy

To translate our vision into reality, DTEB encourages team leadership and creativity at every level in the firm. Our unique effectiveness is the result of our recognizing the specific strength of every member of our design and engineering team and nurturing these core strengths so that our clients can get the best and most innovative design and engineering solutions.

Our Strength

We work closely with our clients and understand client requirements in terms of design and technology as well as budget and deadlines. We offer 100% commitment and we deliver on our promise.

As a result, DTEB’s sophistication in building technology applications and commitment to design quality has resulted in a portfolio that features landmark design accomplishments in Bahrain’s recent urban profile.

05

Quality Services

In order to ensure the utmost level of service to the clients and ensure that projects are implemented to achieve their objectives, it was decided to work towards obtaining various quality certifications.

DTEB is proud that it managed to obtain its ISO 9001:2000 Certification in early 2007. This was achieved after dedicated and hard work of establishing the required detailed procedures to be followed in implementation of works.
